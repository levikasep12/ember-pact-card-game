    EMBER PACT
    A duel of four elements. One card a round. No mercy, no draws.

    Ember burns Gale · Gale scatters Tide · Tide wears Stone · Stone smothers Ember.
    Win a round and your rival loses life equal to the gap. First to 0 life loses the pact.

    VICTORY
    HOW TO PLAY
    • Each round you and Rook each play one card from your hand of five, then draw back up.
    • The card with the higher power wins the round. The loser loses life equal to the difference.
    • Element advantage adds +3 power: Ember > Gale > Tide > Stone > Ember.
    • Mend heals you 4 when played. Ward blocks all damage that round. Siphon heals you for the damage you deal. Wild reveals a random power from 1 to 9.
    • A tie deals no damage. First to 0 life loses. Watch Rook's tell — it hints at what he is planning.
